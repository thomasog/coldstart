# Privacy

coldstart has no server, account or telemetry, and it collects nothing.

What you tell your agent during setup, such as a project's goal or people's names, is written only to files inside your own folder. coldstart doesn't send those files anywhere, and it doesn't read anything outside the folder.

The AI agent you use processes what you share with it under its provider's terms.
